2. What the App does

AlarmStreak is an alarm clock that counts your wake-up only after you complete a task (a “mission”): an exercise recognized by your device’s camera, or an alternative task (a math problem, a photo of an object). The App keeps track of your run of successful mornings (your “streak”).

The App does not guarantee that you will wake up. The alarm depends on the operating system, the state of your device and your own actions. In particular, the alarm may not sound or may not wake you if:

  • the device is turned off, out of battery, restarting or faulty;
  • you have not granted, or have revoked, a permission the alarm needs;
  • you deleted or turned off the alarm, stopped it with the system button, or ignored it;
  • the operating system changed how alarms are scheduled.

Do not rely on the App as your only way to wake up where oversleeping could cause harm or significant loss — including taking medication, caring for others, travel, work, exams and any other commitments at a fixed time. In such cases, use an independent means of waking up. You accept the risk of oversleeping.
