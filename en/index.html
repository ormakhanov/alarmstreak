The iPhone alarm with a mission

Wake up on time. Wake your body up, too.

A morning counts only if you finish a mission within 15 minutes of the alarm: squats or push-ups counted by the camera, math problems, or a photo of a random item at home.

  • Up on time. It rings even on silent and in Do Not Disturb.

  • Morning exercise, built in. 10 squats or push-ups — the camera counts them right on your phone.

  • A streak worth keeping. Every counted morning adds one. Vacation or illness? Pause it.

No cheating yourself

You can turn the alarm off, but the morning only counts if you complete the mission within 15 minutes of the alarm. The camera counts your squats or push-ups, the math has to be solved, the item has to be found.

A streak of mornings

Every counted morning extends your streak. Pause it for a vacation or a sick day, and restore it once every 30 days if your phone lets you down.

Rings even on silent

AlarmStreak uses the iPhone’s own alarm system, so it rings on silent and in Do Not Disturb.

The camera stays on your phone

Video and photos are processed right on your phone and never leave it. No sign-up, no analytics.
