Wake up on time. Wake your body up, too.

AlarmStreak is an alarm for iPhone that counts your morning only after a mission: squats, push-ups, math problems, or a photo of a random item at home.

AlarmStreak Coming soon to the App Store

  1. Up on time. There’s no snooze button, and you have 15 minutes to start the mission.

  2. Mornings start with movement. Choose squats or push-ups — the camera counts all 10 reps for you.

  3. A streak you’ll want to keep. Every counted morning adds one. Days without an alarm and a pause for vacation or illness don’t break it.

You can’t just swipe it away

If you swipe the alarm away or silence it with a volume button, it rings again a minute later, up to three times. If you go to the mission but don’t finish it within 5 minutes, it rings once more.

  1. 2:30 AM
  3. 6:30 AM

No changing your mind half-asleep

The alarm locks 4 hours before it rings. After that, moving it later, turning it off, deleting it or making the mission easier breaks your streak. Getting up earlier or making the mission harder is always fine.

Rings even on silent

No need to turn the sound on or keep the app open: the alarm rings even when your phone is on silent or in Do Not Disturb.

Video never leaves your phone

Squats, push-ups and the item in your photo are recognized right on your phone. Video and photos are never sent anywhere. No sign-up, no analytics.
